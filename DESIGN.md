# Quiet Index

The portfolio is a free horizontal gallery, not a slideshow. Paper, black ink, the cobalt ruler accent, and the illustrated fragments come from [Ricky’s README](https://github.com/0xQuerencia/0xQuerencia/blob/main/assets/project-index.svg). The five-line bio uses DM Sans at one size and weight. The project titles use Newsreader; the artwork and project screenshots stay out of the opening composition.

## Interaction

- [Rauno’s homepage](https://rauno.me/) informed the typographic rhythm, top viewfinder, and sense of pulling back from the page while moving.
- [Invisible Details](https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design) and [Devouring Details: Next.js Dev Tools](https://devouringdetails.com/prototypes/nextjs-dev-tools) informed immediate feedback and reversibility. The track stays native, never snaps or auto-advances, and the camera pulls back during travel, then settles without taking over scrolling. Desktop wheel input moves sideways unless there is vertically scrollable content, and touch/trackpad panning remain native. Ruler targets support taps, drags, keyboard navigation, and links.
- [Field Note 5](https://rauno.me/notes/5) informed the forgiving hit areas and the image itself acting as a link. The image has an explicit open affordance and a keyboard focus ring; hover feedback does not shift its click target.
- [Novelty](https://rauno.me/craft/novelty) informed the first-visit reveal. It plays once per tab session, never blocks input, skips deep links, and is removed with reduced motion. CSS-only transitions are interruptible and do not gate reading or navigation.
